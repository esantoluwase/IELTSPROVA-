IELTS Writing Task 2: Structure & Examples

August 1, 2026  ·  10 min read

IELTS Writing Task 2 is often considered the most challenging part of the IELTS exam. In this guide, I'll show you the exact structure you need to follow, with examples, to achieve a Band 7+ score.

7+

The band score you can achieve with the right structure

1. Understanding the Question

Before you start writing, you need to understand what the question is asking. IELTS Writing Task 2 questions typically fall into one of these categories:

  • Opinion (Agree/Disagree): "Do you agree or disagree?"
  • Discussion (Both Views): "Discuss both views and give your opinion."
  • Advantages/Disadvantages: "What are the advantages and disadvantages?"
  • Problem/Solution: "What are the causes and solutions?"
  • Two-Part Question: "Why is this happening? What can be done?"

2. The Perfect Essay Structure

Here's the structure that works for most IELTS Writing Task 2 essays:

📝 Introduction

Sentence 1: Paraphrase the question
Sentence 2: State your thesis (main idea)
Sentence 3: Outline what you will discuss

📝 Body Paragraph 1

Topic Sentence: State your first main point
Explanation: Explain the point in detail
Example: Provide a specific example
Link: Connect back to the question

📝 Body Paragraph 2

Topic Sentence: State your second main point
Explanation: Explain the point in detail
Example: Provide a specific example
Link: Connect back to the question

📝 Conclusion

Sentence 1: Summarize your main points
Sentence 2: Restate your opinion (if applicable)
Sentence 3: Provide a final thought or recommendation

3. Example Essay

📌 Question:

"Some people believe that technology has made our lives more complicated, while others think it has made our lives easier. Discuss both views and give your own opinion."

📌 Introduction:

Technology has become an integral part of modern life, with its impact being widely debated. While some argue that technology has introduced unnecessary complexity, others believe it has significantly simplified our daily routines. This essay will discuss both perspectives before presenting my own view that technology ultimately enhances convenience and efficiency.

📌 Body Paragraph 1:

Topic: On one hand, technology can complicate life in several ways.
Explanation: For instance, the constant connectivity afforded by smartphones and social media can lead to information overload and reduced face-to-face interaction.
Example: Many people report feeling overwhelmed by the sheer volume of emails, notifications, and messages they receive daily, which can create stress and anxiety.
Link: This suggests that technology can indeed introduce complexity into our lives.

📌 Body Paragraph 2:

Topic: On the other hand, technology has undeniably made many aspects of life easier.
Explanation: Tasks that were once time-consuming, such as shopping, banking, and communication, can now be accomplished instantly from home.
Example: Online banking allows people to pay bills and transfer money in minutes, eliminating the need to visit a physical bank branch.
Link: Therefore, it is clear that technology has brought significant convenience to our daily lives.

📌 Conclusion:

In conclusion, while technology does present some challenges, its benefits in terms of efficiency and convenience are undeniable. In my view, when used mindfully, technology simplifies life and enables us to focus on more meaningful pursuits.

4. Key Tips for a High Score

  • Plan before you write: Spend 5 minutes planning your essay structure
  • Use linking words: "Moreover," "Furthermore," "On the other hand," "In conclusion"
  • Write 250-280 words: More than 250 is good; less is risky
  • Use specific examples: Examples make your argument stronger
  • Check your grammar: Leave time to review your work

⏰ Time Management

Allocate 40 minutes for Task 2 and 20 minutes for Task 1. Use the last 5 minutes to review your answers.
