# IELTS Prova

### AI-Powered IELTS Preparation Platform

IELTS Prova is an online IELTS preparation platform being developed by **Prova Technologies** to help students prepare for the IELTS examination through structured practice, learning resources, progress tracking, and AI-powered learning experiences.

---

## 🚀 Live Product

**[Open IELTS Prova](https://esantoluwase.github.io/IELTSPROVA-/)**

## 🏢 About Prova Technologies

**Prova Technologies** is an emerging technology company focused on building practical AI-powered education products.

IELTS Prova is the company's first major product.

Build accessible technology that helps people learn, prepare, and prove their abilities.

### Motto

---

## 🎯 Product Goal

IELTS Prova is designed to help IELTS candidates prepare across the four major examination areas:

- 📖 Reading
- 🎧 Listening
- ✍️ Writing
- 🗣️ Speaking

The platform aims to provide students with a structured environment for practice and continuous improvement.

---

## ✨ Product Features

The platform is being developed around features such as:

- IELTS practice questions
- Reading practice
- Listening practice
- Writing practice
- Speaking practice
- AI-powered speaking practice concepts
- Student accounts
- Progress tracking
- Target band tracking
- Study tasks
- Vocabulary learning
- Study streaks
- Score tracking
- Premium learning features
