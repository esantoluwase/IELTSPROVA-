🗣️ Part 1

Introduction & Interview — Answer questions about yourself. ⏱️ 4-5 minutes recommended

🎯 Topic: Hometown & Daily Life

  • Where do you live now?
  • What do you like about your hometown?
  • What do you usually do in your free time?
  • Do you prefer to spend time alone or with others?
Sample Response:
"I currently live in a small city called ______. I really like it because it's peaceful and the people are friendly. In my free time, I enjoy reading books and going for walks. I generally prefer spending time with close friends rather than being alone."
💡 Tip: Practice speaking your answers out loud first, then write them down.
🎤 Part 2

Long Turn — You will be given a topic. You have 1 minute to prepare and 2 minutes to speak. ⏱️ 1 min preparation + 2 min speaking

📋 Cue Card

Describe a memorable trip you have taken.

You should say:

  • Where you went
  • Who you went with
  • What you did there
  • And explain why it was memorable
You have 1 minute to prepare. Start speaking when ready.
Sample Response:
"I'd like to talk about a memorable trip I took to the mountains with my family a few years ago. We went to a small village in the countryside during the summer. The journey was long, but the scenery was breathtaking. We spent our days hiking, swimming in the river, and enjoying local food. What made this trip particularly special was that it was the first time my whole family had been together in years. The peaceful environment and quality time with my loved ones created memories that I still treasure today."
💡 Tip: Speak for 2 minutes without stopping. Practice with a timer!
💬 Part 3

Two-Way Discussion — Deeper questions related to the Part 2 topic. ⏱️ 4-5 minutes recommended

🎯 Discussion Questions

  • Why do people enjoy travelling to new places?
  • What are the benefits of travelling with family versus friends?
  • How has technology changed the way people travel?
  • What are the advantages and disadvantages of tourism?
Sample Response:
"People enjoy travelling to new places because it allows them to experience different cultures and break away from their daily routine. Travelling with family can strengthen bonds and create shared memories, while travelling with friends often offers more freedom and adventure. Technology has made travel much easier with apps for navigation, translation, and booking. However, tourism can also have negative impacts, such as overcrowding and environmental damage."
💡 Tip: Give extended answers. Explain your ideas with examples.
