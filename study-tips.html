💡 IELTS Study Tips

Expert strategies to boost your band score in all 4 modules.

📖 Reading

Master Skimming & Scanning

Don't read every word. Learn to:

  • Skim for main ideas (headings, first/last sentences)
  • Scan for specific information (dates, names, numbers)
  • Underline keywords in questions before reading
  • Practice with 20-minute timed sessions
✍️ Writing

Structure Your Essays

Follow this formula for Task 2:

  • Introduction: Paraphrase + Thesis
  • Body 1: Main point + Explanation + Example
  • Body 2: Second point + Explanation + Example
  • Conclusion: Summarize + Final thought
  • Always write 250+ words
🎧 Listening

Listen for Signposts

Key words that signal answers:

  • "First, next, finally" — indicates sequence
  • "However, but" — indicates contrast
  • "For example" — indicates details
  • Practice with 1.25x speed to train your ear
  • Always read questions before the audio starts
🗣️ Speaking

Extend Your Answers

Use the "P.E.E." method:

  • Point: State your main idea
  • Explain: Elaborate with reasons
  • Example: Give a specific example
  • Speak for 2 minutes without stopping in Part 2
  • Record yourself and listen back
📚 Vocabulary

Build Topic-Specific Vocabulary

Learn words by topic:

  • Environment: pollution, conservation, sustainability
  • Technology: innovation, artificial intelligence, automation
  • Education: curriculum, assessment, pedagogy
  • Health: nutrition, epidemiology, wellness
  • Use new words in your writing and speaking
🎯 General

Practice Under Exam Conditions

Simulate the real test:

  • Use our timed tests with no distractions
  • Complete a full test in one sitting
  • Check your answers with our feedback
  • Track your progress on the dashboard
  • Practice daily for 30 minutes
