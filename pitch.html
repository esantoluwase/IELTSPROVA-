The Opportunity

Exam Preparation
Still Has Major Gaps

Millions of learners need affordable, personalized preparation — but much of the experience remains fragmented.

💰

Cost

High-quality tutoring and structured test preparation can be expensive, especially for students in emerging markets.

📚

Fragmented Resources

Learners often combine websites, videos, books and tutors instead of using one integrated preparation experience.

🎯

Limited Personalization

Students need to understand their weaknesses and receive targeted practice instead of repeatedly studying what they already know.

Our thesis: AI can make personalized preparation more accessible, scalable and available on demand.
