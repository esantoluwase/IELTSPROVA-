About Prova

Prova means "to try" in Italian and "proof" in Latin. We help students try until they prove their potential.

Our Story

Prova Technologies was born from a simple belief: every student deserves access to quality test preparation.

We wanted to build something that uses AI, real-time feedback, and personalized learning to help students build confidence and achieve their goals — all for free.

"Success is not about never failing. It's about trying again, learning, and finally proving what you're capable of."

Today, Prova Technologies offers AI-powered practice tests for Reading, Writing, Listening, and Speaking. Students can track their progress, get instant feedback, and improve their scores — all in one beautiful platform.
